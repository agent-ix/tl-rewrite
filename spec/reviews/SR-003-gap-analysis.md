# Gap analysis of tl-rewrite v0.1 candidate

## Summary

Strict coverage reports all requirement rows backed by executable or retained
inspection evidence. Every enabled catalog entry has derivation metadata, an
application fixture, and exhaustive bounded tl-mltl agreement, including a
property population over nonzero Until/Release lower bounds. Ten selected WEST
validation formulas are checksum-pinned, source-bound to typed formula digests,
and independently agree after rewriting. Tests cover stable execution,
reachable graph compaction, every budget, fixed points, cycle detection, replay
substitution, evaluator errors, mismatch counterexamples, wire closure, and
evidence contracts.
Human review and source-release authority remain open. Online-prefix rewriting,
the two growth-sensitive WEST Theorem 3 transformations, universal proof, and
downstream qualification remain explicitly outside the candidate claim.
