# tl-rewrite v0.1 rewrite-engine assurance profile

## Decision Boundary

Evidence supports a human decision about one exact source, catalog, dependency,
and corpus candidate. It does not prove arbitrary new rules, qualify a monitor,
or validate a consuming project.

## Impact Scenarios

The material scenarios are semantic drift, false completion after partial or
unbounded work, and substitution of signal or caller context. Rule derivations,
context-bound replay, binding refusals, negative budget tests, and independent
bounded evaluation detect these before release for the exercised population.

## Evidence Policy

Evidence identifies formula/profile bytes, rule/catalog/dependency revisions,
signal-catalog identity and exact caller context when supplied, budgets, steps,
domains, outcomes, limitations, and requirement tags. Mismatch, unsupported,
exhausted, unresolved-binding, and error states remain non-conclusive. Only
`@kreneskyp` may approve the source release.

## Exceptions

No exception is implicit. A bounded exception must name its owner, affected
rule/criterion, rationale, expiry, evidence impact, and human acceptance record.
