# tl-rewrite v0.1 source-release argument

## Claim

The open claim concerns only the exact source, catalog, dependencies, corpora,
and evidence presented to the human owner. It excludes downstream qualification
and registry publication.

## Reasoning

Complete provenance, deterministic budget, replay, and bounded comparison
obligations reduce uncertainty for the stated use. Human review owns sufficiency.

## Sufficiency Decision

The claim remains open until retained evidence names the exact candidate, every
enabled rule has current evidence, blocking gaps close, and the human owner
records a decision. Automation cannot change this status.

## Challenges

Bounded enumeration and WEST fixtures are not a universal proof of arbitrary
rewrites and cannot qualify a production monitor or consuming project.

The exact `tl-syntax` git-source exception expires on 2026-09-07 or when its
reviewed change merges, whichever comes first. The source-release claim remains
blocked while that exception is open. `tl-mltl` is pinned to its merged `main`
revision. After the remaining upstream merge, this repository must repin the
reviewed immutable revision and regenerate exact-candidate evidence before the
human decision.

Qualification is intentionally host-scoped: `tools.lock` identifies one
declared HOME, cargo target directory, Rust toolchain, launcher set, Node
runtime, and underlying Cargo/Rust compiler binaries. `make ci` is the portable,
manually dispatched workflow gate and does not assert those machine-specific
identities; `make ci-for-evidence` adds the host lock and is valid only on the
declared qualification host. This single-host evidence is a limitation, not a
claim that another machine reproduced the collection. The lock covers the
mandatory language, build, specification, and checksum executables; it does not
claim to identify every operating-system utility invoked by the collector.
