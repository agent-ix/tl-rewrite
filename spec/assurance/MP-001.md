# tl-rewrite v0.1 conformance evidence plan

## Decision Use

Evidence informs only the human source-release decision. It does not itself
approve, prove arbitrary rewrites, qualify monitors, or certify systems.

## Metric and Rule

The metric is the proportion of applicable obligations with a current passing
outcome. The existing all-obligations-must-pass rule is represented by `>= 1.0`;
failed, missing, skipped, invalid, and non-conclusive outcomes stay individually
visible and cannot contribute to the numerator. An empty applicable population
is invalid, not a perfect proportion. The v2 definition adds the numeric rule
and binds the protected measurement apparatus; v1 observations must not be
rejudged under v2. The expected outcomes originate in human-authored
requirements and fixtures, with independent mechanical checks where declared.

## Population

The population is every matrix criterion, catalog entry, budget/error path,
trace field, copied WEST fixture, and declared exhaustive equivalence case.

## Collection Procedure

Record repository CI, strict specification coverage, catalog checks, repeated
rewrites, adversarial budgets, replay substitution, corpus integrity,
horizon-complete enumeration, and code/gap reviews, all against one candidate
revision.

Canonical PGM-01 validation is no longer part of this procedure. It was
performed against the retained evidence archive, which issue #13 deleted under
the authority of `agent-ix/engineering-assurance#7`. It is removed rather than
restated over an empty population.

## Interpretation

An exhaustive agreement is conclusive for its stated pair and domain. It is not
a universal proof schema. Missing dependencies, excess domains, mismatches, and
unsupported profiles block rule-enablement evidence and remain non-conclusive.
